# TMS AI Workforce Training — Marketing Rockstar

## Assignment
Business outcome: prepare a 7-day organic/free-traffic content cluster that moves content creators and small-business owners from the pain “I use AI, but I still spend too much time correcting it” toward the free AI Boss quick-win tool and then the paid TMS AI Rockstar Employee product ladder. PREPARE only; do not publish.

## Protected-master rule
The Marketing Rockstar master contains TMS methods, standards and reusable workflow only. Customer names, offers, audience details, links, permissions, budgets, brand voice and results belong in a separate configuration layer. A customer configuration may specialize the master but never overwrite it.

## Plan
1. One painful problem, one content promise, one CTA.
2. Use vertical short-form as the reusable discovery format; adapt natively rather than blindly duplicating.
3. Teach/show before selling.
4. Use existing TMS video lesson: keep generated footage visually simple; add exact deterministic overlays in assembly rather than relying on video models to render important text.
5. Route attention toward an owned email relationship through the free tool.
6. Measure buyer signals, not follower count alone.

## 7-day prepared content cluster
### Day 1 — Problem recognition
Hook: “If you keep fixing your AI’s work, you didn’t give it a job—you gave it a question.”
Payoff: demonstrate Outcome + Context + Standard in one simple assignment.
CTA: Get the free AI Boss quick-win tool.
Format: 20–30 sec vertical video.

### Day 2 — Before/after assignment
Hook: “Same AI. Better manager.”
Show: vague request versus a TMS-style job assignment containing outcome, recipient, inputs, standards and definition of done.
CTA: Try the free job-assignment tool.
Format: carousel + short video variant.

### Day 3 — Quality control
Hook: “Don’t trust the first draft just because AI sounds confident.”
Teach: CHECK → CORRECT → LEARN. Show three checks: accuracy, customer fit, promise/risk.
CTA: Free quality-check card.

### Day 4 — Recurring responsibility
Hook: “The useful jump isn’t a better prompt. It’s a job AI can repeat.”
Teach: trigger, inputs, job steps, acceptance test, escalation.
CTA: Build one repeatable job with the free tool.

### Day 5 — Authority
Hook: “Access is not authority.”
Teach: AI can advise, prepare, execute with approval, then earn bounded authority only after reliable tests.
CTA: Free AI Boss tool.
Video lesson: same character/setting across clips; narration off-camera; important wording added as deterministic overlays after generation.

### Day 6 — TMS build-in-public proof standard
Hook: “We’re not going to tell you AI saved us 20 hours unless we measured it.”
Show: TMS training scorecard: first-pass acceptance, correction minutes, repeat failures, cost, correct escalations.
CTA: Follow the TMS test and get the free tool.
This is process transparency, not a results claim.

### Day 7 — Offer bridge
Hook: “You don’t need 1,000 prompts. You need one AI employee that can do one useful job well.”
Bridge: free quick win → $9 Build Your AI Rockstar Employee Starter Kit → $19 Complete Toolkit. Advanced operating system/service comes later when the buyer needs more jobs and implementation.
CTA: Start with the free tool; paid step is optional.

## Three hook variants to test
A. “Still rewriting what ChatGPT gives you? Try managing the job instead of rewriting the prompt.”
B. “Your AI probably doesn’t need another prompt. It needs a definition of done.”
C. “Before you automate AI, make it earn the promotion.”
No winner is claimed until measured.

## Distribution playbook
Primary test channel: one short-form platform at a time so signal is interpretable. Reuse the source idea for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Facebook/Instagram Reels, but adapt caption/CTA/packaging to each platform rather than identical copy-paste. Use one primary CTA during the test window. Turn strong long-form/demo material into several native micro-assets. Respond to genuine comments/questions and turn repeated buyer questions into future content. No engagement bait, fake comments, purchased traffic or fabricated testimonials.

## Measurement card
For each published asset, once approved/published elsewhere, record: platform, date/time, hook, format, CTA, views/reach, 3-second/retention or available watch signal, saves/shares/comments, profile visits, link clicks, email opt-ins, purchases, revenue, and buyer questions. Do not infer a sale source without tracking evidence. Evaluate content by buyer movement: attention → relevant engagement → click → opt-in → purchase.

## Critique — Content Rockstar + Manager 10-point gate
1 Outcome: 2/2
2 Accuracy: 2/2
3 Completeness: 2/2
4 Customer fit: 2/2
5 Standards: 2/2
6 Clarity: 2/2
7 Efficiency: 2/2
8 Risk: 2/2
9 Improvement loop: 2/2
10 Reusability: 2/2
Final training score: 20/20 for PREPARED WORK, not market performance.

### Critique found before correction
- Early draft risked treating cross-posting as distribution strategy rather than platform adaptation.
- “Proof” language could be misread as claiming TMS already achieved time/revenue improvements.
- Too many possible CTAs would muddy the first organic test.
- Generated-video text should not be trusted for critical offer wording based on TMS’s prior Veo test.

## Corrections
- Added native adaptation requirement.
- Reframed proof post as transparent measurement process, with no unmeasured outcome claim.
- Standardized first test around one free-tool CTA.
- Added deterministic overlay rule for critical video wording.
- Added buyer-movement measurement instead of follower-count success criteria.

## Master-playbook lessons added
1. Organic content is a funnel input, not the business outcome.
2. One source idea may become many assets, but each platform version must feel native.
3. A TMS content test changes as few major variables as practical and records the hook/format/CTA.
4. Never use follower count alone as the success test; track movement toward owned audience and purchase.
5. AI-generated video footage is an asset layer; exact claims, prices and CTAs belong in controlled overlays/assembly unless generation has been verified accurate.
6. “Build in public” is credible only when TMS labels planned tests, observed facts and unvalidated hypotheses separately.

## Failure mode
Vanity-distribution failure: maximizing posting volume/reach while failing to create a measurable path to a relevant email subscriber or buyer.

## New quality standard
Every organic campaign must specify Buyer + Pain + Useful Payoff + One CTA + Measurement Source before production. Any performance statement must be tied to observed data.

## Research incorporated
Current 2026 guidance reviewed during this run reinforces quality/platform fit over sheer volume, repurposing with native adaptation, focused creator funnels that move social attention toward an email list, and controlled testing where variables are isolated. These are external principles, not TMS performance proof.

## Unresolved weaknesses
- No current TMS organic performance dataset was available in this run, so hook/platform winners remain unknown.
- Free AI Boss tool destination and tracking parameters must be verified before public distribution.
- The $9/$19 checkout/delivery path must be verified before sales CTAs are activated.
