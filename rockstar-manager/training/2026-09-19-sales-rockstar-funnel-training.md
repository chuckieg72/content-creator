# TMS AI Workforce Training — Sales Rockstar

## Specialist trained
Sales Rockstar

## Manager assignment
Create a buyer-focused sales funnel for the TMS AI Boss Starter Kit that turns the painful problem “I use AI, but I still do too much of the work” into a measurable path from free value to a paid first purchase, without overpromising results.

## Business outcome
Produce a reusable funnel brief and email sequence that can be handed to Marketing Rockstar and Content Rockstar for traffic and creative production.

## Research inputs used
- Current 2026 lead-magnet guidance favors hyper-specific resources, fast consumption, low-friction opt-ins, and a clearly planned follow-up path rather than generic long PDFs.
- Current funnel guidance supports a sequence of awareness → lead magnet → nurture → paid offer → next step, but benchmark claims were treated as planning ranges, not TMS results.
- Existing TMS lessons: use the buyer → pain → useful solution → quick win/proof → trust → offer sequence; distinguish tested lessons from unvalidated ideas; use deterministic overlays for important video copy; keep customer configuration separate from the protected master agent.

## Deliverable produced
### Offer path
1. Free lead magnet: **5-Minute AI Boss Upgrade**
   - One-page outcome-first assignment card
   - One worked before/after example
   - One self-check question
2. Entry offer: **Build Your AI Rockstar Employee — Starter Kit** at recommended test price **$9**
   - B.O.S.S. method
   - 10–15 job tools
   - quality scorecard
   - recurring responsibility builder
   - 3-run proving-ground challenge
3. Core offer: **TMS AI Employee Complete Toolkit** at **$19**
   - broader operating system, authority ladder, promotion path, reusable job cards
4. Advanced offer: future **AI Business Operating System** and premium implementation/service

### Funnel promise
“Give AI a job, a standard, and a way to improve—so you spend less time fixing generic work.”

### Landing-page structure
- Headline: **Still fixing the work AI was supposed to save you?**
- Subhead: Get the 5-Minute AI Boss Upgrade and turn one repeated task into a clearer, testable AI assignment.
- Bullets: define the outcome; add context; set a standard; make AI check its work.
- Proof language: “This is the same management pattern TMS is using while building its own AI workforce.”
- CTA: **Get the free AI Boss Upgrade**
- Friction rule: request email only at first capture; qualify later.

### 5-email nurture sequence
**Email 1 — Deliver + quick win**
Subject: Your AI Boss Upgrade is inside
Give the asset, explain the one-job test, ask the reader to use it on one recurring task, and invite a reply with the task they chose.

**Email 2 — Name the hidden problem**
Subject: The problem may not be the AI
Explain that one-off prompting creates repeated correction work. Contrast “ask for an answer” with “assign an outcome and acceptance test.” No sales pitch beyond a soft mention of the Starter Kit.

**Email 3 — Show the management pattern**
Subject: What a real AI job assignment includes
Teach outcome, context, standards, authority, self-check, and improvement loop. Link to a short TMS proof/demo when available; do not claim performance results that have not been measured.

**Email 4 — Introduce the paid implementation step**
Subject: Ready to build one Rockstar Employee?
Present the $9 Starter Kit as the shortcut for turning the free one-page tool into a complete job. Emphasize practical worksheets and examples, not “magic prompts.”

**Email 5 — Trust + next step**
Subject: Make AI earn more responsibility
Explain the promotion path: test → supervise → trust → schedule. Offer the $19 Complete Toolkit as the next step for people who want the full system. State that human review remains required for important work.

### Measurement plan
Track, without inventing results:
- landing-page visitors
- email opt-ins
- delivery/open/click signals when available
- replies naming a real task
- $9 purchases
- $19 upgrades
- common objections and failure points

## Manager critique against TMS 10-point quality gate
1. Outcome: 2/2 — clear funnel output tied to a real TMS priority.
2. Accuracy: 2/2 — no fabricated results; research ranges treated as external guidance only.
3. Completeness: 2/2 — includes offer ladder, landing page, sequence, metrics, and handoff.
4. Customer fit: 2/2 — aimed at creators and small-business owners correcting AI output.
5. Standards: 1/2 — needs a stronger explicit acceptance test for the lead magnet itself.
6. Clarity: 2/2 — simple language and distinct next steps.
7. Efficiency: 2/2 — reuses existing TMS positioning and assets; no unnecessary new product.
8. Risk: 2/2 — avoids unsupported guarantees and keeps human-review language.
9. Improvement: 1/2 — measurement plan exists, but first test conditions need to be specified.
10. Reusability: 2/2 — can be adapted to other TMS products by swapping the pain, job, and offer.

**Initial score: 18/20**

## Corrections made
1. Added lead-magnet acceptance test:
   - A first-time user must be able to complete one real AI assignment in 5–10 minutes.
   - The asset must produce a visible improvement in specificity or repeatability, even if business impact is not yet measured.
2. Added first-test conditions:
   - one landing page
   - one CTA
   - one email-only opt-in
   - one traffic source at a time
   - no paid promotion
   - stop and revise if the page attracts clicks but no meaningful task replies or if the offer is misunderstood.
3. Clarified that “less time fixing work” is the desired outcome, not a validated claim.
4. Preserved the master-agent boundary: customer details, examples, and tool permissions belong in a configuration layer and must not overwrite the Sales Rockstar master.

## Final score
**19/20 — training pass with one unresolved dependency: actual live funnel execution and measured response data.**

## Reusable lesson added to the master playbook
A low-cost AI product should not lead with “more prompts.” It should lead with a specific recurring job, a visible quick win, and a clear promotion path from free assignment card to paid operating system.

## Failure mode recorded
**Funnel abstraction failure:** promising an “AI business system” before showing the buyer one concrete job they can assign today. Corrective standard: every funnel asset must show the first job, the first standard, and the first test.

## Quality standard added
Every TMS AI funnel must include:
- one painful specific buyer problem;
- one immediate job-level quick win;
- one low-friction capture step;
- one paid next step that expands implementation rather than repeating the free asset;
- explicit “not yet validated” language wherever proof is not available.
