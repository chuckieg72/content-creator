# TMS AI Workforce Training Lab v0.1

## Objective
Make the master agents measurably better before customer deployment through recurring realistic work.

## Daily training cycle
1. Select the next specialist using rotation.
2. Assign a realistic business outcome.
3. Require the specialist to plan before execution.
4. Produce a usable deliverable.
5. Have the Manager or another specialist critique it.
6. Score it on the 10-point TMS gate:
   Outcome, Accuracy, Completeness, Customer Fit, Standards, Clarity, Efficiency, Risk, Improvement, Reusability.
7. Correct failures.
8. Record the lesson and reusable standard.
9. Create the next test based on the weakest area.
10. Never mark an untested claim as proof.

## Specialist rotations
- Sales: offers, funnel, email, lead follow-up, objections.
- Marketing: positioning, campaigns, audience, channel strategy.
- Ads: Meta/Facebook campaign architecture, creative testing, measurement.
- Content: hooks, scripts, thumbnails, repurposing, retention.
- Research: source quality, synthesis, opportunity detection, actionable intelligence.
- Product: product creation, packaging, pricing logic, customer usability.
- Operations: SOPs, recurring jobs, reporting, handoffs, exception handling.

## Cross-agent tests
At least periodically:
- Sales creates an offer; Marketing critiques positioning.
- Marketing creates a campaign; Content turns it into assets.
- Ads creates a campaign test; Sales critiques conversion alignment.
- Research supplies findings; Product turns them into a product hypothesis.
- Operations designs the workflow; Manager checks authority and failure handling.
- Manager assigns an end-to-end campaign and evaluates the whole team.

## Learning record
For each test capture:
- assignment
- agent
- output
- score
- critique
- corrections
- failure mode
- reusable lesson
- updated standard/playbook
- next test

## Graduation
A specialist is not promoted merely because it produced a good answer. Promotion requires repeated successful tests, clear failure handling, appropriate authority boundaries, and evidence that improvements persist in later assignments.

## Customer readiness
Before commercialization, use the master team on TMS itself. Separate:
- verified capability
- tested internal result
- planned capability
- customer-specific configuration

Do not claim a customer result that TMS has not validated.
