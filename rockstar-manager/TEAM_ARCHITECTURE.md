# TMS AI Workforce — Master Team Architecture v0.2

## Purpose
Build a reusable TMS master AI workforce that can be deployed repeatedly for different businesses without rewriting the master agents.

## Manager
**Rockstar Manager** owns outcomes, delegation, tool selection, quality control, approvals, learning, and escalation.

Core loop:
REQUEST → ASSIGN → PLAN → EXECUTE → CHECK → CORRECT → APPROVE/DELIVER → LEARN → REPEAT

## Master specialists
1. Sales Rockstar — offers, funnels, lead generation, follow-up, sales copy, conversion work.
2. Marketing Rockstar — positioning, campaigns, email, social, audience and channel strategy.
3. Ads Rockstar — Meta/Facebook ad strategy, creative variations, testing plans, measurement.
4. Content Rockstar — YouTube, Shorts/Reels, hooks, packaging, repurposing.
5. Research Rockstar — market, competitor, customer-language, trend, tool and opportunity intelligence.
6. Product Rockstar — digital products, packaging, pricing, product ladders and improvements.
7. Operations Rockstar — SOPs, recurring workflows, reporting, documentation and process improvement.

## Master vs customer configuration
The TMS master agents contain reusable skills, standards, playbooks, evaluation methods and original TMS frameworks.

Customer instances add a separate configuration layer:
- business and offer information
- audience
- brand voice
- goals
- geography
- processes
- connected tools/accounts
- permissions
- approval rules
- performance targets

Customer configuration must never overwrite the TMS master.

## Authority ladder
1. ADVISE — recommendations only.
2. PREPARE — produces work ready for review.
3. EXECUTE WITH APPROVAL — executes approved actions.
4. EXECUTE WITHIN LIMITS — executes defined low-risk workflows within an Authority Card.
5. MANAGE BY EXCEPTION — runs recurring responsibilities and escalates only exceptions.

Promotion path:
TEST → SUPERVISE → TRUST → SCHEDULE

## Training principle
Each master specialist receives recurring realistic jobs, critique, correction and learning. The goal is not model retraining; it is continuous improvement of the agent's instructions, playbooks, examples, standards and workflow knowledge.

## Customer deployment
Customer questionnaire → AI Employee Blueprint → specialist selection → customer configuration → tool/account matching → Authority Card → onboarding → supervised tests → deployment.

## Non-negotiable controls
No purchases, billing changes, public publishing, consequential external communication, security/account changes, or irreversible actions without the required authority and approval.
Never fabricate results, proof, customer outcomes or validation.
